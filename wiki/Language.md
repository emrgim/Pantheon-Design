# Language

**English only** on slides, site chrome, speaker notes, stamps, buttons, alt text that is UI.

Keep Latin **names**: Iuppiter, Iuno, Minerva, Vesta, Ceres, Diana, Venus, Mars, Mercurius, Neptunus, Volcanus, Apollo, Saturnus, Ianus, Dis, Fortuna, Bacchus, Bellona, Quirinus, Tellus, Veritas, Libertas, Iustitia.

## Cover placeholders

- `PANTHEON · TEMPLATE`
- `Presentation title`
- `Subtitle · client · context`
- `Place · date · version`
- Footer `dei.mrg.im` · stamp `PANTHEON · MRG.IM`

Forbidden on chrome: Titolo, Sottotitolo, Luogo, Grazie, Agenda as Italian UI (the English word “Agenda” is fine).
