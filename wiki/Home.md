# Pantheon graphic system

One set. Two surfaces: **dei.mrg.im** and **Google Slides**.

## Non-negotiable

1. **One blue.** `#1E5197`. No second blue. No cyan. No navy. No electric glow.
2. **White type** on that blue — four steps, never a fifth hue.
3. **English** on every chrome string, placeholder, stamp, footer, speaker note.
4. **Photos of the gods stay.** Do not recolor, regenerate, or crop-replace catalog portraits.
5. **No amber** (`#FFAC02`) on UI chrome. Phosphor is Hermes-only on icon packs.
6. Product name is **Pantheon**. Not Olympus.

## Feeling

A steel plate painted with industrial enamel. The figure is worn, sfumato, built like a **blueprint**: faint construction lines and tiny source-code grain, not a marble statue and not a neon CRT.
