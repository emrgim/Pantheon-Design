# Pantheon graphic system

Canonical spec for **Pantheon** visuals only: [dei.mrg.im](https://dei.mrg.im), Pantheon Google Slides, and video of Pantheon or of the catalog gods.

This repo is **not** the design system for Crewforce, BuddyPro, Giano, Giunone, Saturno, Mercurio product UI, BASE 211, or any other brand.

## Non-negotiable

1. **One blue.** `#1E5197`. No second blue. No cyan. No navy. No electric glow.
2. **White type** on that blue — four steps only.
3. **English** on every chrome string, placeholder, stamp, footer, speaker note, on-screen title.
4. **Catalog photos stay.** Do not recolor, regenerate, or replace `/renders` portraits on dei.mrg.im.
5. **No amber** (`#FFAC02`) on UI chrome. Phosphor is Hermes-only on icon *packs*, never on site/slides chrome.
6. Product name is **Pantheon**. Not Olympus / OLYMPVS.
7. Figure on a plate: **same scale, more faded** (sfumato). Not smaller. Not neon.

## Feeling

A steel plate painted with industrial enamel `#1E5197`. The image is worn into the paint like a **blueprint**: construction lines and tiny source-code grain forming the god or the building. Not a marble statue in a room. Not a CRT.
