# Type

Load **IBM Plex Serif**, **IBM Plex Sans**, **IBM Plex Mono**.

| Role | Face | Size (slides 16:9) | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover title | Serif | 40 pt | 600 |
| Section title | Serif | 40 pt | 600 |
| Slide title | Serif | 28 pt | 600 |
| Quote | Serif | 28 pt | 400 |
| Subtitle | Serif | 20–22 pt | 400 |
| Body / list | Sans | 16 pt | 400 |
| Caption | Sans | 12–14 pt | 400 |
| Stamp / footer | Mono | 11 pt | 500 |
| Slide number | Mono | 11 pt | 400 |

Site: Serif on `h1`/`h2`/god names. Sans on body. Mono on `.stamp`, OLP refs, codes.

Tracking on stamp: slight, uppercase: `PANTHEON  ·  MRG.IM`. Footer: `dei.mrg.im`.

Do not use Inter, Roboto, or system UI as the brand face.
